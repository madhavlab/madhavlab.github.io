## Overview

- Collaboration with JSS Institute of Speech and Hearing, Mysuru 

## Objective

To develop AI-powered diagnostic and assistive tools for individuals with learning disorders such as dyslexia and speech/hearing impairments, supporting accessibility in education and healthcare through cutting-edge speech and audio processing technologies. 

## Key Achievements & Expected Impact

__Innovative Technology Development__: 

* Developing __speech assessment__ tools using advanced AI and natural language processing (NLP) methods. 

* Building __adaptive learning systems__ that offer personalized educational support for individuals with dyslexia and other learning disorders. 

__Field Validation and Collaboration__: 

* Conducting __real-world clinical validation__ through collaboration with speech and hearing experts. 

* __Field studies__ involving students and individuals with speech impairments to ensure practical usability and impact. 

__Societal Impact Focus__: 

* Targeting __inclusive education__ by providing AI-powered solutions for __personalized learning__, speech therapy, and __diagnostic support__. 

* Applications envisioned in __schools, clinics, and public sector programs__ to benefit students and individuals with learning disabilities across India. 

* AI-based __assistive tools, speech therapy modules, and diagnostic platforms__ in multiple Indian languages (Hindi, Kannada, English, and growing). Deployed and tested for 100s of school students. 

__Anticipated Deliverables__: 

* AI-based __assistive tools, speech therapy modules, and diagnostic platforms__. 

* __Publications__ in top AI and healthcare venues, contributing to academic excellence and practical societal benefit.
