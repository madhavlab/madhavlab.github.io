For student intern, short-term research (after BTech) and postdoc positions.

### Topics

- Baevski, A., Zhou, Y., Mohamed, A., & Auli, M. (2020). wav2vec 2.0: A framework for self-supervised learning of speech representations. Advances in neural information processing systems, 33, 12449-12460.
- Ho, J., Jain, A., & Abbeel, P. (2020). Denoising diffusion probabilistic models. Advances in neural information processing systems, 33, 6840-6851.

### How to apply
If you are comfortable understanding the above papers, 
send an email to Prof. Arora with the heading "[UG Project Appl] \<Project Name\>" or "[Postdoc Appl] \<Project Name\>".
We are open for students outside IITK too.

### Guidelines
- No PVF without tangible progress. 
- At least two semesters of work for LoR.
