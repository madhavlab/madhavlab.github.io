Vipul Arora is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in ESAT (Electrical Engineering) at KU Leuven. His research interests include probabilistic machine learning and its applications to audio analysis, speech recognition, and computational Physics. He received his B. Tech. and PhD degrees in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur, India. He did a postdoc at Oxford University (UK), and then, worked at Amazon Alexa in Boston (USA). Then, he served as an Assistant Professor and later an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Kanpur.
